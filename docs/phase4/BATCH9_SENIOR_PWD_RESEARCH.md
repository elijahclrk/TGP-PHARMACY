## Legal findings and source register

### R01 — Senior eligibility, coverage and non-stacking

**VERIFIED / high.** RA 9994 §3 (amended RA 7432 §2) and §4 (amended §4(a)(i), proof-of-entitlement and promotional-discount paragraphs); accompanying IRR Art. 5.1/5.5 and Art. 9: resident Filipino Seniors aged 60+, including the stated dual-citizenship/residency conditions, receive the medicine benefit for their exclusive use. OSCA ID, Philippine passport and qualifying age/citizenship documents are recognized; do not enforce an OSCA-only dropdown. The statute provides 20% discount and VAT exemption where applicable. A promotional offer and statutory discount are alternatives, not cumulative discounts. Authorized representation is recognized. Approval: 15 February 2010; §13 effectivity is 15 days after the specified publication, whose first date was not independently established here. [RA 9994 and IRR](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2010/ra_9994_2010.html).

### R02 — Senior medicine procedure; historical clauses requiring overrides

**VERIFIED / high, subject to subsequent amendments.** DOH AO 2010-0032 V.I(b), V.III(a)(2), V.IV(a), VI and VII: VAT is removed before the statutory discount; the illustrated PHP 100 becomes PHP 71.43. Prescription is required except for OTC medicines; ordinary dispensing is generally limited to one month's supply, and OTC supply may be limited within the stated seven-day ceiling. Partial prescription fulfillment is a dispensing duty. VI contains historical supplier-sharing rules; do not invent a branch allocation from them. VII states retroactivity to 6 July 2010. Issued 9 October 2010. Its booklet clauses are amended by R03. Its V.II(b) statement excluding PWD VAT relief predates RA 10754 and is not a current PWD rule. [Official FDA-hosted AO](https://www.fda.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Administrative-Order-No.-2010-0032.pdf).

### R03 — Senior medicine purchase booklet removed

**VERIFIED / high.** Actual DOH AO 2024-0017 is reproduced in the official BOC CMC 10-2025 attachment, PDF pages 7–8 (zero-based 6–7). It deletes AO 2010-0032 V.III(a)(3), replaces V.IV(a)'s partial-filling paragraph, deletes AO 2012-0007 V.6.5, VI.3.7 and VI.6.1(c), and replaces VI.3.3's partial-filling paragraph. Pharmacists record filled quantity and remaining balance on the prescription; the Senior retains the partly filled prescription. Other unaffected provisions remain. Final paragraph: immediate effect. Issued **23 December 2024**. Do not require a Senior medicine booklet or convert it into a mandatory digital equivalent. This is not a PWD-booklet repeal. [DOH order as officially circularized by BOC](https://customs.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/2025CMC-10-2025.pdf), corroborated by [NCSC's 24 December 2024 explanation](https://www.ncsc.gov.ph/post/a48e7c6b).

### R04 — FDA implementation and vitamin/mineral scope

**PARTIAL / medium:** FDA Circular 2025-005's final existence and booklet-delisting subject are verified in the official circular index; the official four-page PDF is accessible but image-only in the research interface. Its exact paragraph numbering, signature/publication dates and independent effectivity date are not asserted. The March 2025 draft is not law. R03 supplies the readable operative removal. [FDA circular index](https://www.fda.gov.ph/category/fda-circular/), [final official PDF](https://www.fda.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/FDA-Circular-No.2025-005.pdf).

**VERIFIED / high:** FDA Advisory 2020-1547 expressly reproduces AO 2012-0007 Part IV §3.1 as amended by AO 2012-0007-A: coverage includes medically prescribed vitamin/mineral supplements for prevention/treatment. Do not classify all supplements as excluded, or all shelf vitamins as automatically eligible. Verify the actual product and prescription evidence. The advisory does not restore subsequently removed booklet requirements. Exact publication-based effectivity of the original amendment was not established. [FDA Advisory 2020-1547](https://www.fda.gov.ph/fda-advisory-no-2020-1547-reiteration-of-doh-administrative-order-no-2012-0007-as-amended/).

### R05 — PWD entitlement is distinct from Senior entitlement

**VERIFIED / high.** RA 10754 §1, amending RA 7277 §32(a)(3), grants at least 20% discount and VAT exemption if applicable on medicine purchases for the PWD's exclusive use. Approval: 23 March 2016; §6 specifies effectivity 15 days after the prescribed publication. First publication date not independently established here. [RA 10754, official NCDA republication](https://ncda.gov.ph/disability-laws/republic-acts/republic-act-no-10754-an-act-expanding-the-benefits-and-privileges-of-persons-with-disability-pwd/).

**VERIFIED / high:** IRR §§5.1, 11–12 recognize Filipino PWD beneficiaries, qualifying dual/reacquired citizenship, LGU/PDAO IDs, the passport route for apparent disability and NCDA emergency IDs. Do not impose a Senior age threshold on PWDs or independently diagnose disability at checkout. One person qualifying under both schemes uses one benefit. IRR signed 1 December 2016; §16 has publication/ONAR conditions, exact completion date unverified. [Official IRR](https://ncda.gov.ph/disability-laws/implementing-rules-and-regulations-irr/irr-of-ra-10754-an-act-expanding-the-benefits-and-privileges-of-persons-with-disability-pwd/).

### R06 — PWD documentary, representative and dispensing requirements

**VERIFIED / high; later repeal not located, current SOP confirmation required.** DOH AO 2017-0008 VI.4(A)(i–iii), VI.4(B)(i–iii): ID evidence, prescription except OTC, purchase booklet, representative authorization/current date and representative ID; special guardian/representative arrangements for minors or persons unable to authorize. Prescription information includes beneficiary, medicine/form/strength/quantity and prescriber identifiers. General dispensing limit is one month; OTC may be limited to one week. Partial fills are recorded in the booklet/special record and remaining prescription; fully filled prescriptions are surrendered under this text. Drugstores maintain a special PWD record book. VI.1 also addresses covered devices and food for special medical purposes; ordinary nonmedicine merchandise is not automatically covered. VII repeals AO 2009-0011. Issued 1 June 2017; VIII: effective after publication in two newspapers, exact dates unverified. [Official DOH order on NCDA](https://ncda.gov.ph/disability-laws/administrative-orders/doh-ao-2017-0008-implementing-guidelines-of-republic-act-10754-otherwise-known-as-an-act-expanding-the-benefits-and-privileges-of-persons-with-disability-for/).

No verified 2025–2026 nationwide instrument removing the **PWD medicine** booklet was located. A proposal to remove it is not an enacted repeal. Obtain the branch's current DOH/NCDA/PDAO instruction before activation; never copy the Senior removal across schemes.

### R07 — PWD calculation, sales evidence and income-tax distinction

**VERIFIED / high.** BIR RR 5-2017 §§3.3, 4, 5.3–5.9, 6 and 7: generic/branded medicines are covered; VAT-inclusive PHP 1,120 is reduced to PHP 1,000 then discounted PHP 200, leaving PHP 800. For non-VAT sellers, do not remove nonexistent VAT. No Senior/PWD double discount. Separate accurate records include beneficiary name/ID, gross sale, discount, date and invoice number. Covered exempt and taxable sales must be distinguishable. The income-tax example reports VAT-exclusive gross PHP 1,000 and separately addresses discount deductibility; PHP 800 cash collection is not the same metric. Eligibility for an itemized deduction is not automatic under OSD. Dated 11 April 2017; §13: 15 days after prescribed publication, exact first publication unverified. EOPT invoicing updates below supersede older receipt terminology. [Official RR 5-2017 on NCDA](https://ncda.gov.ph/disability-laws/implementing-rules-and-regulations-irr/revenue-regulations-no-5-2017-rules-and-regulations-implementing-republic-act-no-10754/).

### R08 — Independent product VAT exemption

**VERIFIED / high for statutory basis.** RA 11534 §12 amends NIRC §109(1)(AA) for prescribed medicines for specified diseases; this is a product exemption, distinct from beneficiary entitlement. Its dates and categories are not a universal medicine exemption. Historical COVID-related provisions must not be carried forward as an unrestricted current list. [CREATE Act](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2021/ra_11534_2021.html).

**VERIFIED / high for circular; SKU application UNVERIFIED.** RMC 87-2026, issued/indexed **4 August 2026**, publishes the FDA letter dated **4 May 2026** and updated list: cancer 708, diabetes 331, high cholesterol 172, hypertension 537, kidney disease 152, mental illness 300, tuberculosis 77; total 2,277. Its final paragraphs supersede inconsistent earlier RMCs and retain effect until subsequent endorsed updates. RMC 27-2026 (8 April 2026) is the earlier list, not the final located reference. [RMC 87 full text](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2087-2026_redacted.pdf), [earlier RMC 27](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2027-2026.pdf).

**PARTIAL / medium:** [official Annex A](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2087-2026%20Annex%20A%20(6).pdf) is linked by the BIR release but exceeded retrieval size. No individual entry, SKU mapping or Annex-specific effective date is certified. RMC 87 is the latest consolidated BIR medicine list located in this review, not a guarantee that no later FDA endorsement exists. Recheck official updates at classification approval and activation. A disease label or generic-name substring is insufficient evidence. Do not strip VAT again from an already exempt selling price.

### R09 — Current invoice basis and mixed transactions

**VERIFIED / high.** RA 11976 §21, amended NIRC §113(A–B), requires VAT invoices and separate VAT/exempt/zero-rated treatment; mixed sales need an explicit breakdown or separate invoices. An exemption is not zero rating. Approval: 5 January 2024; effectivity **22 January 2024** is confirmed by RMC 77's introductory statement. [EOPT Act](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html).

RA 11976 §33, amended NIRC §235, sets preservation of books/subsidiary/accounting records at **five years**, reckoned using the return-filing deadline or late-filing rule for the last-entry taxable year. This is not a five-years-from-sale automatic deletion rule. Map supporting evidence to the applicable tax records and any continuing legal hold; the accountant/privacy reviewer must approve the actual retention schedule. The same [EOPT Act](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html) is the authority, rather than unqualified older ten-year guidance.

**PARTIAL / medium for complete form inspection:** RR 7-2024 §§3(B), 6(A–B), 8 is the base registration/invoice rule; its official PDF is image-only here. Readable official RMC 77 Q6 cross-refers to §6(B). Do not certify a final invoice template from an unchecked scan. [RR 7-2024](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf).

**VERIFIED / high from official indexed text:** RR 11-2024 §1 replaces RR 7 §8 transition provisions; do not reuse RR 7's original deadlines unamended. The manual/loose-leaf conversion provisions are not permission to call an arbitrary new web printout an authorized invoice. BIR digest lists issue date 13 June 2024; signature date and independent effectivity not asserted. [Full RR 11](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%2011-2024.pdf), [BIR digest](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%2011-2024.pdf).

**VERIFIED / high from readable indexed official passages:** RMC 77-2024 Q1, Q6, Q7, Q24, Q26 and Q30 clarify invoicing: VAT sellers issue per sale, invoice content follows RR 7 §6(B), mixed amounts are segregated, and ordinary official receipts are supplementary. Q30 confirms **27 April 2024** effectivity of the invoicing provisions. Dated 11 July 2024. Do not perpetuate expired machine-conversion transitional relief as a 2026 authorization. [Official RMC 77](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2077-2024.pdf), [official digest](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2077-2024%20Digest.pdf).

### R10 — 2026 withholding-tax invoice amendment

**VERIFIED / high.** RR 6-2026 §2 adds §6(B)(22), a separate creditable withholding-tax amount when applicable; §§3/6 address transition/effectivity. Dated 25 August 2026; BIR index/publication is 1 October 2026; effectivity clause is 15 days after first OG/BIR publication. [RR 6-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%206-2026.pdf).

RMC 112-2026 I–III, especially II.4–9 and III.A, clarifies actual applicability: CWT is not a sales discount or reduction in gross/VAT sales. Ordinary B2C retailers need not ask every customer about withholding; nonwithholding transactions need no invented deduction. Template/transition duties depend on invoice type. Dated 12 October; issued/indexed 22 October 2026. First-publication completion and any specific system-reconfiguration deadline must be checked against its annex for the chosen invoice method. [RMC 112 full text](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%20112-2026_redacted.pdf).

### R11 — 2026 Senior discount burden-sharing amendment

**VERIFIED / high for operative rule; calendar commencement PARTIAL.** RR 7-2026 §2 amends RR 7-2010 §§2(m), 4(a.2), 7(7)/(9). Pharmaceutical supply-chain parties may enter documented DOH-governed sharing arrangements; each claimant's deductible expense cannot exceed its own share. The regulation does not authorize reducing the customer's mandated benefit or create a universal 50/50 allocation. Itemized versus OSD treatment matters. Dated 17 September 2026; BIR issue/index date **13 October 2026**. §5: 15 days following first OG/BIR publication. Exact first publication not independently verified; if 13 October was first, the computed date is 28 October, not the signature date. Do not book supplier receivables or automatic tax deductions without the branch's actual agreement and accountant review. [RR 7-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-2026_redacted.pdf), [BIR 2026 RR index](https://www.bir.gov.ph/2026-Revenue-Regulations?label=2026&to=2026-Revenue-Regulations&type=PAGE).

### R12 — Electronic invoicing coverage must be assessed

**VERIFIED / high from official digests; full clause numbering/branch coverage PARTIAL.** RR 11-2025's electronic-invoice coverage and transitory provisions, as amended by RR 26-2025 (amended §6 quoted in BIR's digest), distinguish covered CAS/CBA/invoicing-software, LTS/large and specified e-commerce taxpayers from POS-only categories subject to further conditions. The amended compliance date for named covered groups is **31 December 2026**; not every internal web application is e-commerce. Head-office/branch relationships can affect coverage. A printed PDF is not automatically structured electronic invoicing. Issued 27 February and 16 October 2025 respectively; complete initial publication dates unverified. [BIR RR 11 digest](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%2011-2025.pdf), [BIR RR 26 digest](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%2026-2025%20Digest.pdf).

**PARTIAL / medium:** the current official index lists RMC 98-2026, 22 September 2026, concerning electronic invoice policies under these regulations. Complete text could not be retrieved; assess it before deciding applicability or transmission scope. Do not assume software registration or exemption. [BIR 2026 RMC index](https://www.bir.gov.ph/2026-Revenue-Memorandum-Circulars?label=2026&to=2026-Revenue-Memorandum-Circulars&type=PAGE).

### R13 — Updated PWD identification

XIII–XIV establish a unified digital/QR identification system, validity/revalidation rules, three-year transition and amendment of the 2021 order. Issued 2 June 2026; official article dated 9 September; effectivity requires Board approval and lawful publication, whose exact completion date was not established. Do not hardcode all IDs to a numeric format, require a printed card for every digital ID or require a visible disability. Treat this as agency operational guidance, not an invented cancellation date for every legacy card. [PNA report quoting NCDA](https://www.pna.gov.ph/article/ncda-trains-13k-lgus-for-unified-digital-pwd-id-rollout).

### R14 — Identification privacy and retention

**VERIFIED / high.** RA 10173 §§3(l), 11–13, 16 and 20 cover sensitive identity/health data, proportionality, lawful processing, rights and security. Mandatory tax evidence does not authorize general disclosure. The lawful basis for sensitive information needs the appropriate §13 condition; do not rely solely on generic legitimate-interest language. Retention is purpose/legal-obligation based, not automatically perpetual; erasure is not an excuse to destroy records still lawfully required. Approved 15 August 2012; §45 specifies 15 days after the stated publication, exact first publication not independently established. [NPC's official Act](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/).

## Reconciliation and unresolved legal details

| Topic | Design consequence and status |
| --- | --- |
| Senior versus PWD | Separate documentary policies. R03 overrides Senior booklet text only. R05/R06/R13 apply to PWD evidence, including minors/representation/ID transition. |
| Eligibility versus tax | Independent scheme-specific eligibility and product/branch tax evidence. A covered medicine not on the general VAT list may still receive beneficiary-specific relief; a generally VAT-exempt medicine is not made discount-eligible merely by being on that list. |
| Unknown classifications | Block the benefit quote/posting, identify affected lines, permit a deliberate return to ordinary cash flow without silently removing a claimed entitlement. Escalate classification review, not routine sale approval. |
| Promotion | R01/R05/R07 prohibit stacking. The older AO's PWD VAT exclusion is obsolete. Exact promotional candidate/tax interaction needs approved accounting policy; no universal multiply-two-discounts algorithm. |
| Precision | R02/R07 establish order and illustrative arithmetic. No reviewed source settled every cent-level product-line/header/FEFO rounding tie. The existing helper's half-up intermediate rounding is a proposed policy awaiting accountant sign-off, not a proven statutory universal. |
| Representative verification | PWD requirements are explicit in R06. Senior representation is recognized in R01; obtain the current consolidated AO 2012-0007/A and final FDA circular before declaring exact Senior authorization-document exceptions. Do not impose PWD guardianship requirements on Seniors. |
| Record books and prescriptions | A POS record does not automatically replace a required physical PWD booklet, special pharmacy record or dispensing endorsement. Confirm accepted electronic substitutes and partial-fill SOP. |
| Reporting | Cash collected, VAT-exclusive prediscount gross, discount granted, exempt/taxable bases and deductible discount share are separate metrics. Reports must not label cash collection as taxable gross or deductibility certification. |
| Local facts | VAT registration, selling-price basis, product evidence, dispensing procedure, invoice authorization/series, software coverage, supplier-sharing contracts and authorized reviewers cannot be inferred from public law. |

## Currentness checks and limits

Reviewed official 2026 RR/RMC indexes through the located November entries, searched post-August FDA/BIR list updates and 2025–2026 DOH/NCDA/NCSC documentary changes. No later consolidated BIR medicine list or nationwide PWD-booklet repeal was verified. This is a bounded search result, not proof that none exists. Do not infer effectivity from a crawl timestamp, PDF upload folder or signature alone. Acquire legible final FDA 2025-005, the consolidated Senior procedure, the complete current Annex A and RMC 98 before activation. Public sources do not identify TGP's actual registration or classifications.

**Verdict:** sufficient to define guarded implementation work and precise unresolved gates; **not sufficient to activate Senior/PWD posting or fiscal invoices**. Ordinary cash POS remains the tested baseline. No law or public article substitutes for branch evidence and pharmacist/accountant validation.
