## 3. Proposed transaction and UI state machine

1. **Select Regular / Senior / PWD.** Regular retains the existing API/storage behavior. A benefit selection is enabled only after server availability validates installed schema, approved policy, branch/invoice configuration and required code capabilities. Current availability stays false.
2. **Enter identity.** Beneficiary name and presented document number remain mandatory, trim-only normalization, Unicode support, lengths 200/100 matching preparation validation. Capture document type/issuer and relevant validity evidence. Do not force all IDs to numeric format or a fixed expiration; digital/legacy PWD transition is handled by a versioned SOP (R01/R05/R13).
3. **Document verification.** An authenticated actor records identity/exclusive-use attestation, prescription status/reference, prescriber evidence where needed, requested/remaining integer quantity and actual dispensing verifier. Separate Senior and PWD checklists (R02–R06). OTC does not silently mean every vitamin or device is prescription-exempt. Representative mode captures verified relationship/authority and minimal protected reference data; PWD guardian exceptions and Senior procedures are distinct. No fabricated registry integration or arbitrary ID-upload requirement.
4. **Resolve each cart line on server.** Read effective reviewed eligibility separately for the chosen scheme, intrinsic VAT classification, price basis/version, prescription category and policy applicability. Explicitly ineligible is different from unknown. Unknown rejects the benefit quote with affected product IDs and safe explanations; no estimated totals or cash posting. Ineligible products can remain in an otherwise validated mixed basket.
5. **Determine price/tax basis.** Use branch VAT status and actual item selling-price basis. Never divide a known exempt/non-VAT price by 1.12. Verify intrinsic exempt product prices were configured correctly; the benefit feature must not silently repair regular prices.
6. **Calculate.** Server runs the approved deterministic algorithm below, never browser-supplied rates, net totals or eligibility. Promotions require an independently reviewed non-stacking policy; an already promotional price with unknown provenance blocks the statutory candidate, rather than applying another 20%.
7. **Cashier review.** Show item name/generic/form/strength, quantity, original price, eligibility decision/reason, original tax classification, final transaction tax treatment, VAT removed, discount base/rate/amount and payable. Show gross, VAT removal, discount, taxable/exempt/non-VAT bases, VAT and final total. Mask IDs outside the editing task. A generic total alone is insufficient.
8. **Review becomes stale** after identity/scheme, item/quantity, price, date, FEFO allocation/expiry, classification/evidence, policy, document status or invoice configuration changes. Clear reviewed state; require a fresh signed quote and explicit review, preserving the current closed-attempt recovery protocol. Never silently accept the changed total.
9. **Cash payment.** Show tender and change against the reviewed server total. Require explicit final confirmation. One atomic transaction performs the steps below; no routine approval queue entry.
10. **Immutable completion.** Save the full financial/beneficiary/document/policy/invoice snapshots before commit. Return the persisted receipt projection; do not reprice it from current catalog metadata.
11. **History/reporting.** Paid sales are immediately recognizable only after reconciliation. Show returns/disposition separately; do not infer cash movements from physical goods returns. Provide accurate historical financial columns and permitted exports.
12. **Retry/unknown outcome.** Preserve request identity and an opaque recovery reference, lock the cart while unresolved, query outcome, and replay the original receipt if committed. Do not offer a new request UUID until the earlier reference is authoritatively completed or closed.

## 4. Proposed calculation contract

### Input and policy

`calculateBenefit(beneficiary, verifiedLogicalLines, approvedBranchPolicy)` accepts only server-resolved data. Monetary amounts are decimal strings, never JS binary floats. Positive integer base-unit quantities, unique product/cart keys and finite nonnegative price with at most two decimals are required. Existing money parser supports up to 12 whole digits; enforce the same operational cap on tender/line/header amounts before SQL, reject overflow rather than silently expand bounds.

Policy snapshot includes scheme, statutory discount basis points, applicable VAT basis points, branch VAT registration, approved price-basis map, calculation version, rounding version, effective interval and source/evidence approvals. Arithmetic examples use **2,000 discount bps and 1,200 VAT bps** under the stated VAT-registered scenario; these are not defaults for unclassified inventory. Lower-level arithmetic can be configurable, but only approved exact statutory policies may be activated. A cashier/Admin cannot set an arbitrary legal rate.

Define `HU(n/d) = floor((2*n+d)/(2*d))` for nonnegative integer n,d; it returns integer cents and implements half-up. Proposed version `half_up_product_line_v1` is the existing helper's approach made explicit. **Accountant sign-off is required**; R02/R07 examples do not establish every rounding tie.

### Logical product line calculation in cents

Let `G = quantity * original_unit_price_cents`.

| Verified circumstance | Algorithm |
| --- | --- |
| VAT-registered branch, eligible, price includes VAT and intrinsic classification vatable | `B=HU(G*10000/(10000+vatBps)); R=G-B; D=HU(B*discountBps/10000); N=B-D`. Final sale is beneficiary-exempt: exempt base=N, taxable base=VAT=0. |
| Eligible, already intrinsically VAT-exempt with exempt selling-price basis | `B=G; R=0; D=HU(G*discountBps/10000); N=G-D`; exempt base=N, VAT=0. No second VAT removal. |
| Eligible, verified non-VAT branch and non-VAT price basis | `B=G; R=0; D=HU(G*discountBps/10000); N=G-D`; non-VAT base=N, VAT=0. Never relabel the product's intrinsic tax evidence as a general product exemption. |
| Ineligible, VAT-registered, known VAT-inclusive vatable item | `R=D=0; N=G; taxable_base=HU(N*10000/(10000+vatBps)); VAT=N-taxable_base`. |
| Ineligible, known intrinsically exempt item | `R=D=VAT=0; N=G; exempt_base=N`. |
| Ineligible, verified non-VAT branch | `R=D=VAT=0; N=G; non_VAT_base=N`. |
| Unknown, expired/unapproved evidence, unsupported tax/basis or missing branch status | No financial quote, no posting; controlled unavailability identifying unresolved line(s). Unknown must not be coerced to ineligible or vatable. |

`G = VAT_removed + discount + N` and `N = taxable_base + exempt_base + non_VAT_base + VAT` must hold per logical line, allocated batch line and header. Original classification and final treatment are separate snapshot fields. VAT_removed is not a discount; neither is CWT. For statutory exempt lines, original VAT rate may be snapshotted for the removal calculation while VAT charged is zero.

For regular legacy sales, preserve existing recognition and leave unrecorded historical tax unknown. Do not manufacture prior tax amounts from today's classifications. A future fully classified regular snapshot is distinct from an unclassified legacy cash sale.

### FEFO batch allocation and rounding

Calculate once for each logical product/benefit/tax/price group, then allocate to original FEFO batches. Do not rerun rounding independently for every batch: physical stock distribution must not alter the customer's reviewed total.

Use deterministic **largest remainder** allocation for each cent component, ties in FEFO order then batch_id/sale_item_id. First allocate VAT removal proportional to original gross, bounded by allocated gross; then discount proportional to remaining base, bounded by that base; derive net. Allocate remaining tax components within their net capacities and derive the residual. Store logical-to-batch links and allocation/rounding version. Reconcile every sum. Original `sale_items.unit_price` remains the regular price; never overwrite it with a divided rounded net price.

Example: 3 units at PHP 0.01 on an eligible VAT-inclusive logical line yield G=.03, B=.03, D=.01, N=.02 under this proposed policy. Splitting into batches of 1 and 2 units allocates discount .00/.01 and net .01/.01. Independently calculating three one-unit discounts would yield .03 payable and is forbidden. A later return references the actual sold batch/snapshot; it does not retroactively recalculate a discount or generate a refund.

### Promotions and partial quantities

Select exactly one beneficiary scheme. Do not compute successive Senior and PWD discounts. If promotions exist, compare independently valid candidate prices and tax treatments under an approved policy; do not apply the statutory rate to an already-discounted unknown price or compare percentages with incompatible VAT bases. No general promotion workflow is being added. If its tax treatment or price provenance is unresolved, block that benefit candidate until clarified.

Partial prescription fulfillment means a positive integer number of supported base units within remaining prescribed quantity; it does not authorize fractional-tablet inventory. Reject 0, negative, fractional, excessive or duplicate quantities. Fractional unit-of-measure conversion requires separate approved inventory/schema work. Lock a local prescription balance when recording partial fills. An internal ledger cannot establish purchases at other pharmacies; external documentary checks remain a pharmacist/SOP responsibility.
