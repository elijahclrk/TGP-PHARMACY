## Limits to state openly

- This is an academic/local functional demonstration using synthetic policies and identities. Production features are not activated and no signed owner/pharmacist UAT or legal certification is claimed.
- A **transaction confirmation**, even printed or exported to PDF, is an internal system record. It is **not a registered fiscal invoice**, proof of tax-system registration or approved electronic invoicing. Physical printer/paper acceptance is separate.
- Full physical receipt of each accepted return is supported; incremental physical receipt is not. Partial *requested/sold* quantities are supported.
- Automatic package conversion is unavailable without confirmed per-SKU units, package ratio, price/cost basis, allowed fractions and rounding. Strength/form does not supply those facts.
- Lower-value/statutory/discounted/prescription exchanges and automatic replacement-return handling remain gated. The no-refund preference does not decide applicable legal remedies.
- No general patient profile/full prescription register, live statutory policy editor, automatic Admin recovery, purchasing rejection/cancellation or fabricated external-resolution register is offered.
- Docker runtime, hosting, independent backup/restore, hosted isolated CI, representative load/pilot outcomes and real human training/acceptance remain unverified. Source/build checks are not those operational results.
